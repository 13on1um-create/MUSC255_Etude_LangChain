MUSICAL GAME.
ALLEGRANDO.
Instruction and Pleasure Combined.
A SPLENDID GAME FOR EVERY HOME.

This game consists of a board on which the different notes and rests are printed, one on every card. After a number are distributed among the players, the cards are played in succession and added together as they are played, until the value of a whole note is reached, when the counters one for the person who played the last card takes that pile. The board or score is marked from 1 to 7, and the first to reach the seventh hole wins the game. For the Piano, Organ, Violin, or any other instrument. The game is intended chiefly for schools, and is perfectly adapted to the use of private families. It not only teaches the value of the notes and rests, but also trains the eye and memory, and is in fact, all that could be desired to introduce a child to reading music.
